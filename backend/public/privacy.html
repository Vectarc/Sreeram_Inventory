Privacy Policy

Sree Ram Dyes & Chemicals — Multi-Branch Inventory Suite

Last Updated: June 24, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Sree Ram Dyes & Chemicals (SRDC) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Sree Ram Company mobile application (the "App").

The App is designed and deployed as an internal enterprise tool to facilitate branch management, inventory balance sheet tracking, transaction logs, and vendor directory accessibility for Sree Ram Dyes & Chemicals.

2. Information We Collect

To ensure secure authentication, operational accountability, and inventory transparency, the App collects the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Business email addresses, usernames, roles, and encrypted passwords created by system administrators to restrict unauthorized system access.
  • Inventory & Transaction Data: Records of stock updates, transfers between commercial branches, stock audits, adjustments, and vendor contacts.
  • System Audit Logs: Access logs, login timestamps, and username associations to maintain operational security compliance.

3. Device Permissions Required

For the App to function correctly, it requires access to certain native hardware and software utilities on your mobile device:

  • Camera Camera Access: Used exclusively to capture high-compression photographs of physical products to upload to the inventory catalog.
  • Photo Library Photo Library / Gallery Access: Used to select and upload stored product photos into the inventory catalog.
  • Internet Network Connectivity: Used to synchronize data in real-time with our secure private backend cloud servers.

Note: Device permissions are only requested when you attempt to capture or select a photo. You can enable or disable these permissions at any time through your device's native settings panel.

4. How We Use the Data

We use the collected information solely to power the business operations of Sree Ram Dyes & Chemicals, including:

  • Authenticating authorized administrative and staff members.
  • Maintaining accurate, real-time stock levels and low-stock notification triggers.
  • Ensuring transparent ledger entries for commercial branch audits.
  • Sending security-related One-Time Passwords (OTP) via SMTP server for admin account password recovery.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement enterprise-grade technical security measures to protect your database assets:

  • All communications between the App and the server are encrypted using HTTPS (SSL/TLS).
  • Authentication uses industry-standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Inventory, transaction histories, and user roles are stored in a secure cloud database restricted to company networks.
  • We do not share, lease, sell, or disclose any collected information to third-party advertisers, marketers, or external companies.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights

As this is an internal enterprise application, account registration and removal are managed centrally by the system administrator.

If you wish to deactivate your profile, purge your account registration, or request removal of transaction history association, please contact your branch administrator or email us directly at the support address below. Requests will be processed in accordance with internal corporate policies.
